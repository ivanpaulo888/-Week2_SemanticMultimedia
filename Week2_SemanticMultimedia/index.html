About Rene

Rene Clert Baterbonia, along with 21-year-old Nigerian teammate Divine Adili, drowned during a team-building exercise at Hermanos Leisure Farm and Surf Camp in Dipaculao, Aurora, Philippines. The activity involved walking and jogging in seawater approximately 300 meters from the resort, despite warnings about strong currents and tides. Both athletes were swept away by sudden rip currents around 2:30 p.m., and their bodies were recovered 30 to 40 minutes later after rescue efforts by teammates and emergency responders. CPR was performed, but both were declared dead on arrival at Aurora Memorial Hospital in Baler .
